## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 What Is the HOS Marketplace?

House of Spells (HOS) Marketplace is a multi-vendor online shopping platform built for the fandom community. It brings together officially licensed merchandise, independent artisan sellers, and curated collections from popular fictional universes — all in one place.

Think of it as a shopping destination where fans of Harry Potter, Lord of the Rings, Star Wars, and countless other franchises can find unique products, earn loyalty rewards, and connect with a vibrant community.

The platform is not just an online shop. It also powers the House of Spells physical retail stores (currently in the UK and the NYC Times Square flagship), creating a seamless experience between online and in-store shopping.

### 1.2 How to Access the Platform

| What You Want to Do | Where to Go |
|---|---|
| Visit the landing page and learn about House of Spells | **houseofspells.com** |
| Browse and shop the marketplace | **shop.houseofspells.com** |
| Join the loyalty program (in-store QR enrollment) | **join.houseofspells.com** |
| Visit a seller's dedicated storefront | **{seller-name}.houseofspells.com** (e.g., wizardwares.houseofspells.com) |

All you need is a modern web browser on any device — desktop, tablet, or phone. No app download is required.

### 1.3 Overview of User Roles

The platform supports 14 different roles. Each role determines what a person can see and do. Here is a plain-English summary:

| Role | Who Is This Person? | What Can They Do? |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer** | A shopper / buyer | Browse products, place orders, join the loyalty program, write reviews, track orders, submit returns |
| **Seller (B2C)** | An individual or small business selling products | List products for sale, manage orders from buyers, track earnings, customize their storefront |
| **Seller (Legacy)** | An older seller account type | Same as B2C Seller — this role exists for backward compatibility |
| **Wholesaler** | A business-to-business seller | Sell products in bulk, offer B2B pricing, manage wholesale orders |
| **Admin** | A platform administrator | Full access to everything — user management, products, orders, finance, settings, reports |
| **Influencer** | A content creator or affiliate partner | Generate product links, earn commissions on sales, manage a personal storefront |
| **Store Staff** | An employee at a physical HOS store | Look up loyalty members, enroll customers, process ship-from-store orders, redeem loyalty vouchers at the till |
| **Procurement** | A procurement team member | Review and approve/reject product submissions from sellers |
| **Fulfillment** | A warehouse / shipping team member | Manage shipments, fulfillment centers, and delivery logistics |
| **Catalog** | A catalog management team member | Enrich product data, manage catalog entries, organize taxonomy |
| **Marketing** | A marketing team member | Create campaigns, manage promotions, prepare marketing materials |
| **Finance** | A finance / accounting team member | Oversee pricing, payouts, settlements, fee reports, and revenue reports |
| **CMS Editor** | A content editor | Manage website pages, blog posts, banners, and media |
| **Sales** | A sales team member | View users, orders, sellers, and products for sales support |

### 1.4 Logging In

1. Go to **shop.houseofspells.com/login**
2. Enter your email address and password
3. Click **Sign In**

You can also sign in with:
- **Google** — Click "Sign in with Google"
- **Facebook** — Click "Sign in with Facebook"
- **Apple** — Click "Sign in with Apple"

If you forget your password, click **Forgot Password** on the login page. You will receive an email with a reset link that is valid for **60 minutes**.

After logging in, you are automatically taken to the dashboard for your role (for example, the Admin Dashboard if you are an admin, or the Customer Dashboard if you are a customer).

## 2. Customer Journey

This section describes the full experience of a shopper on the HOS Marketplace — from first visit to order delivery and beyond.

### 2.1 Registration

New customers can create an account in two ways:

**Option A: Email Registration**

1. Go to **shop.houseofspells.com/register**
2. Enter your first name, last name, email address, and a password
3. Optionally enter your phone number and country
4. Agree to the Privacy Policy
5. Click **Create Account**
6. Check your email for a verification link and click it to activate your account

**Option B: Social Login**

1. Go to **shop.houseofspells.com/login**
2. Click one of the social login buttons (Google, Facebook, or Apple)
3. Authorize House of Spells to access your basic profile information
4. Your account is created and linked to your social profile

After registering, you will be asked to complete a **Fandom Challenge** — a quick verification question that confirms you are a real fan and helps personalize your experience.

### 2.2 Fandom Challenge

The Fandom Challenge is a required step during customer registration (both online and through the in-store enrollment flow at join.houseofspells.com). Here is how it works:

1. A multiple-choice question about a popular fandom is displayed (e.g., "What is the name of Harry Potter's owl?")
2. Select your answer from the four options
3. You can click "Try a different question" to get a new question
4. Both the challenge token and your answer are required — the registration will not proceed without them
5. Your answer helps build your **Fandom Profile** — a personalized summary of which fictional universes you love most

The Fandom Profile is used behind the scenes to show you relevant products, promotions, and content.

> **Note:** The Fandom Challenge is separate from **Fandom Quizzes** (see Section 10.3), which are optional trivia games you can play anytime to earn loyalty points.

### 2.3 Browsing and Searching

Once on the marketplace, you can find products in several ways:

**Browse by Category**
- Use the top navigation menu to explore product categories (e.g., Wands, Apparel, Home Décor, Collectibles)

**Browse by Fandom**
- Visit **shop.houseofspells.com/fandoms** to see all available fictional universes
- Click on a fandom (e.g., Harry Potter, Star Wars) to see all products from that universe

**Browse by Seller**
- Visit **shop.houseofspells.com/sellers** to see all marketplace sellers
- Click on a seller to visit their dedicated storefront

**Search**
- Use the search bar at the top of any page
- Type a product name, character, fandom, or keyword
- Results appear instantly as you type (powered by smart search technology)
- Filter results by price range, fandom, category, seller, rating, and more
- Sort by relevance, price (low to high or high to low), newest, or popularity

**Browse by Collection**
- Visit **shop.houseofspells.com/collections** to see curated product lists
- Collections are themed groupings (e.g., "Gifts Under $25," "Best of Hogwarts," "New Arrivals")

### 2.4 Product Details

When you click on a product, you see:

- **Product images** — multiple high-quality photos that you can browse through
- **Product name and description** — what the product is and what makes it special
- **Price** — the selling price in your selected currency
- **Variants** — options such as size, color, or style (if applicable)
- **Stock availability** — whether the item is in stock
- **Fandom and category** — which universe and product type it belongs to
- **Customer reviews and ratings** — what other buyers thought of the product
- **Seller information** — who is selling this product
- **Related products** — similar items you might also like
- **Social sharing** — buttons to share the product on social media

### 2.5 Wishlists

Save products you love for later:

1. Click the **heart icon** on any product card or product detail page
2. The item is added to your wishlist
3. View all your saved items at **shop.houseofspells.com/wishlist**
4. From your wishlist, you can:
   - Move items to your cart
   - Remove items you no longer want
   - Share your wishlist

### 2.6 Cart and Checkout

**Adding Items to Your Cart**

1. On a product page, select any variant options (size, color, etc.)
2. Choose the desired quantity
3. Click **Add to Cart**
4. A confirmation appears and you can continue shopping or go to your cart

**Reviewing Your Cart**

1. Click the cart icon in the header or go to **shop.houseofspells.com/cart**
2. Review all items, quantities, and prices
3. Update quantities or remove items as needed
4. Enter a **coupon code** or **promo code** if you have one and click Apply
5. See the order subtotal, shipping estimate, and taxes

**Checkout Flow**

Step 1: Cart Review → Step 2: Shipping Address → Step 3: Shipping Method → Step 4: Order Review → Step 5: Payment

1. **Shipping Address** — Enter or select your delivery address
2. **Shipping Method** — Choose from available shipping options (standard, express, etc.)
3. **Order Review** — Confirm all details are correct
4. **Payment** — Enter your payment details

**Payment Options**

| Payment Method | How It Works |
|---|---|
| Credit / Debit Card | Enter card details securely via Stripe |
| Gift Card | Enter your gift card code to apply the balance to your order |
| Loyalty Points | If enrolled in The Enchanted Circle, redeem points for a discount (see Section 3) |
| Combined Payment | Use a gift card or loyalty discount, then pay the remaining balance by card |

After payment, you receive:
- An **order confirmation** on screen
- A **confirmation email** with your order number and details

### 2.7 Guest Checkout

If guest checkout is enabled, visitors can place orders without creating an account. The process is similar but requires entering an email address for order communications. An account can be created after the order is placed.

### 2.8 Order Tracking

After placing an order, you can track it in several ways:

**From Your Account**

1. Go to **My Orders** (shop.houseofspells.com/orders)
2. Click on any order to see its status and timeline
3. View shipping information, tracking numbers, and estimated delivery dates

**Quick Track (No Login Required)**

1. Go to **shop.houseofspells.com/track-order**
2. Enter your order number and email address
3. View the current status of your order

**Order Statuses Explained**

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Pending | Your order has been placed and is awaiting processing |
| Confirmed | The seller has confirmed your order |
| Processing | Your order is being prepared for shipment |
| Shipped | Your order is on its way — a tracking number is available |
| Delivered | Your order has been delivered |
| Cancelled | Your order was cancelled (by you or the seller) |
| Returned | A return has been processed for this order |

### 2.9 Returns

If you need to return a product:

1. Go to **My Orders** and find the order
2. Click **Request Return** on the order detail page
3. Select the items you want to return
4. Choose a reason for the return
5. Add any additional comments
6. Submit the return request

What happens next:
- The seller reviews your return request
- If approved, you receive instructions on how to send the item back
- Once the returned item is received and inspected, a refund is processed
- The refund goes back to your original payment method

You can check the status of all your returns at **shop.houseofspells.com/returns**.

### 2.10 Customer Dashboard

Your personal hub at **shop.houseofspells.com/customer/dashboard** gives you quick access to:

| Section | What You'll Find |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Update your name, email, phone, and password |
| **Orders** | View all your past and current orders |
| **Purchases** | Complete purchase history |
| **Wishlist** | All your saved items |
| **Collections** | Curated product collections you've created |
| **Gift Cards** | View your gift card balances |
| **Downloads** | Access any digital products you've purchased |
| **The Enchanted Circle** | Your loyalty dashboard (points, tier, rewards) |
| **Quests** | Fun challenges to complete for rewards |
| **Leaderboard** | See how you rank against other members |
| **Notifications** | Updates about your orders, promotions, and more |
| **Support** | Access the knowledge base or submit a support ticket |

## 3. Loyalty Program — The Enchanted Circle

The Enchanted Circle is the House of Spells loyalty and community ecosystem. It rewards customers for shopping, engaging, and being part of the fandom community — both online and in physical stores.

### 3.1 Program Overview

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Program name | The Enchanted Circle |
| Earn rate | 1 point per $1 spent (base rate, before tier multipliers) |
| Point value | 100 points = $1 |
| Minimum redemption | 100 points |
| Points expiry | 24 months from the date earned |
| Works at | Online (shop.houseofspells.com) and all HOS physical stores |

### 3.2 Enrollment

There are multiple ways to join The Enchanted Circle:

**Online Enrollment**

1. Log in to your customer account
2. Go to **The Enchanted Circle** from your dashboard (shop.houseofspells.com/loyalty)
3. Click **Join the Program**
4. You are instantly enrolled and receive **100 welcome points**

**In-Store Enrollment (QR Code)**

1. At any House of Spells store, scan the QR code displayed at the till or entrance
2. The QR code takes you to **join.houseofspells.com**
3. Enter your first name, last name, email, phone (optional), and create a password
4. Answer a fun **Fandom Challenge** question
5. Acknowledge the Privacy Policy
6. Tap **Join in Seconds**
7. Your digital loyalty card appears immediately on screen — show it to staff at the till

**In-Store Enrollment by Staff**

1. Store staff can enroll a walk-in customer from the Store Portal
2. Staff enters the customer's email and name
3. The customer receives an email to complete their profile

**Referral Enrollment**

1. If someone shares their referral link with you (e.g., houseofspells.com/ref/HOS-FRIEND-A7F2), clicking it sets the referral code
2. When you sign up, both you and the referrer earn bonus points

### 3.3 The Six-Tier System

Members progress through tiers based on their **total lifetime points earned** (not their current balance). Tier reviews happen weekly (every Sunday).

| Tier | Name | Points to Reach | Earning Multiplier | Key Benefits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Initiate** | 0 (sign-up) | 1.0× | Welcome bonus, birthday reward, member pricing |
| 2 | **Spellcaster** | 1,000 lifetime | 1.25× | Free standard shipping, early sale access (12 hours) |
| 3 | **Enchanter** | 3,000 lifetime | 1.5× | Free express shipping, early product drops (24 hours), priority support |
| 4 | **Dragon Keeper** | 7,500 lifetime | 2.0× | Free worldwide shipping, exclusive products, in-store event priority |
| 5 | **Archmage Circle** | 15,000 lifetime | 2.5× | VIP events, personal shopping advisor, first access to collaborations |
| 6 | **Council of Realms** | Invite-only (top 1%) | 3.0× | Annual gathering, product co-creation, dedicated relationship manager |

**Important tier rules:**
- Customers can only move **up** — they never auto-downgrade to a lower tier
- Council of Realms is invite-only and cannot be reached by points alone
- Tier multipliers apply on top of the base earn rate (e.g., a Dragon Keeper earns 2 points per $1 instead of 1)

**Example:** A Spellcaster (1.25× multiplier) who spends $50 earns 63 points (50 × 1.25 = 62.5, rounded up).

### 3.4 Earning Points

Points are earned through purchases and engagement activities:

**Purchase Points (Automatic)**

| Scenario | How Points Are Earned |
|---|---|
| Standard online purchase | 1 point per $1 spent (multiplied by tier rate) |
| In-store purchase at HOS outlet | 1 point per $1 spent (multiplied by tier rate) |
| Purchase from opted-in third-party seller | 1 point per $1 spent (at the seller's configured rate) |
| Purchase from seller with loyalty disabled | No points earned |

Points are credited when the order is marked as paid. For in-store purchases, points typically appear within minutes when the POS sale is imported.

**Engagement Points**

| Activity | Points Earned | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Sign up / enroll | 100 | Once |
| Complete your profile | 50 | Once |
| Write a product review | 25 | 3 per month |
| Submit a photo review | 50 | 3 per month |
| Share a product on social media | 10 | 5 per day |
| Refer a friend (referrer bonus) | 500 | Unlimited |
| Referred friend bonus (on first purchase) | 200 | Once |
| In-store QR check-in | 15 | 1 per day per store |
| Attend an in-store event | 100 | Per event |
| Complete a Fandom Quiz | 25 | Weekly |
| Birthday bonus | 200 | Once per year (automatic) |
| Membership anniversary | 150 | Once per year (automatic) |
| Founding member bonus | 500 | Once (for early registrants) |

**Campaign and Bonus Points**

Admins can configure special bonus campaigns, such as:
- **Double Points Weekend** — a multiplier active during a specific date range
- **Brand Partnership Campaigns** — boosted earn rates on products from a specific brand (e.g., "2× points on all Harry Potter merchandise this week")
- **Product Campaigns** — extra points on specific products or categories
- **Click & Collect Bonus** — flat bonus points when choosing click & collect (if enabled)

### 3.5 Redeeming Points

**At Online Checkout (Web)**

1. Add items to your cart and proceed to checkout
2. If you are enrolled and have at least 100 points, the **Loyalty Redemption Widget** appears
3. Choose a reward from the catalogue:

| Reward | Points Required | Value |
|---|---|---|
| $1 Discount | 100 | $1 off your order |
| $5 Discount | 500 | $5 off your order |
| $10 Discount | 1,000 | $10 off your order |
| Free Shipping Upgrade | 200 | Free shipping on this order |
| Raffle Entry | 50 | Entry to a prize draw |
| $5 Gift Card | 500 | A $5 gift card code |
| $1 Charity Donation | 100 | Donated on your behalf |
| Early Access Pass | 300 | Early access to new product releases |

4. Each redemption requires a unique confirmation key to prevent accidental double-redemptions
5. Points are deducted and the discount is applied to your order total
6. Pay the remaining balance (if any) by card or gift card

**At a Physical HOS Store (In-Store POS)**

1. Tell the staff member you would like to redeem loyalty points
2. Staff looks up your account by loyalty card number, email, or phone
3. Staff initiates a **POS voucher redemption** — this converts your loyalty points into a till credit
4. The credit is applied to your in-store purchase at the register
5. Minimum redemption: $1 (100 points); Maximum: $500 per voucher

**Via the Rewards Page (Online)**

1. Go to **shop.houseofspells.com/loyalty/rewards**
2. Browse available rewards
3. Select a reward and confirm
4. Discount redemptions generate a one-time coupon code valid for 30 days

### 3.6 Digital Loyalty Card and QR Code

Every Enchanted Circle member receives a **digital loyalty card** with a unique QR code:

1. Go to **shop.houseofspells.com/loyalty/card**
2. Your card displays:

Earn bonus points by inviting friends to join:

1. Go to **shop.houseofspells.com/loyalty/referral**
2. Find your unique referral code (format: HOS-XXXX-XXXX)

The Ambassador Program extends the referral system for highly engaged members:

Control how The Enchanted Circle communicates with you:

1. Go to **shop.houseofspells.com/loyalty/preferences**

## 4. In-Store Operations

This section is for **Store Staff** working at House of Spells physical retail locations. It covers the daily tools and processes used at the till and in the back office.

### 4.1 Store Staff Portal Overview

When a Store Staff member logs in, they see the **Store Dashboard** (shop.houseofspells.com/store) with five key actions:

| Action | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Ship a Purchase** | Start shipping an in-store purchase to a customer's address |
| **Pending Customers** | View customers who still need to register or add their address |
| **Back Office Packing** | Receive paid orders, check items, weigh packages, generate carrier labels |
| **Loyalty Member Lookup** | Search for loyalty members by card, email, phone, or name |
| **Enroll Walk-In** | Register a walk-in customer for The Enchanted Circle at the till |

The dashboard also shows live counters:
- **Waiting for details** — customers who need to complete their shipping information
- **Awaiting payment** — orders with a quote sent but payment not yet received
- **In packing** — orders in the back office ready to be packed and shipped

### 4.2 Loyalty Member Lookup

To look up a customer's loyalty account:

1. Go to **Store → Loyalty Member Lookup** (shop.houseofspells.com/store/lookup)
2. Search by any of the following:
   - Loyalty card number
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Customer name
3. The system displays the customer's:
   - Name and contact information
   - Current tier and points balance
   - Available rewards
4. From here, you can:
   - Enroll the customer in The Enchanted Circle (if not already enrolled)
   - Initiate a points redemption (POS voucher)

### 4.3 Loyalty Enrollment at the Till

To enroll a walk-in customer:

**Quick Method (QR Code)**

1. Point the customer to the QR code at the till or store entrance
2. The customer scans it with their phone and is taken to **join.houseofspells.com**
3. They fill in their details, answer the Fandom Challenge, and tap "Join in Seconds"
4. Their digital loyalty card appears on their phone — they can show it immediately

**Staff-Assisted Method**

1. Go to **Store → Enroll Walk-In** (shop.houseofspells.com/store/enroll)
2. Enter the customer's email address and name
3. Submit the enrollment
4. The customer receives a welcome email to complete their profile

### 4.4 POS Voucher Redemption (Redeeming Points In-Store)

When a customer wants to use their loyalty points for an in-store purchase:

1. Look up the customer via **Loyalty Member Lookup**
2. Confirm their identity and points balance
3. Initiate a **POS Voucher Redemption**
4. Enter the dollar amount to redeem (minimum $1, maximum $500)
5. The system converts points to a Lightspeed gift card value
6. The gift card is loaded onto the POS (Lightspeed)
7. Apply the gift card as payment (or partial payment) for the customer's purchase at the register
8. The customer's points balance is reduced accordingly

**One-Time Password (OTP) Verification:** For security, high-value redemptions may require the customer to confirm via a one-time code sent to their email or phone.

### 4.5 Ship-From-Store

The "Ship a Purchase" flow lets store staff arrange shipping for items a customer bought in-store. This is especially useful for tourists and customers who don't want to carry large items.

**The complete flow involves two people working together: Staff at the till and the Customer on their phone.**

**Step 1: Staff Creates the Shipping Order**

1. Go to **Store → Ship a Purchase** (shop.houseofspells.com/store/shipping)
2. Confirm the correct Store ID is selected (it is locked for store staff)
3. Type or **scan the receipt barcode** to enter the Lightspeed invoice number
4. If the invoice has a customer email on file, it is used automatically
5. If no email is on file, type the customer's email address manually
6. Tick the **Customer consent** checkbox
7. Click **Create Shipping Order**
8. A **QR code and magic link** are generated — share with the customer

The order receives a unique HOS order number (format: HOS-STORECODE-DDMMYY-NNNN, e.g., HOS-NYC-290926-0001).

**Step 2: Customer Completes Their Details (On Their Phone)**

The customer opens the magic link from their email or scans the QR code. The claim link is **single-use** — once claimed, it cannot be reused.

1. Customer signs in (or creates an account if they don't have one)
2. Their email must match the email on the Lightspeed invoice
3. Customer saves their profile (name, phone)
4. Customer adds a shipping address
5. Customer assigns items to the shipping address:
   - **Single destination** — all items go to one address
   - **Multiple destinations** — items can be split across different addresses (e.g., one shipment to the US and one to the UK as a gift)
   - **Carry in hand** — mark items the customer is taking with them (these are excluded from shipping)
6. Customer confirms their item assignments

**Step 3: Staff Quotes the Box Charge**

1. The staff order page auto-refreshes every 5 seconds
2. When the customer's address groups appear, staff selects a **box size** for each shipment group (Small, Medium, Large, XL, or Custom)
3. The shipping price is determined by the box size and destination tier:

| Tier | Countries |
|---|---|
| Domestic US | United States, Puerto Rico |
| Near-International | Canada, Mexico, Caribbean |
| Western Europe & UK | United Kingdom, France, Germany, etc. |
| Rest of World | All other countries |

4. Staff clicks **Finalize Quote** to send the price to the customer
5. All prices are in **USD**

**Step 4: Payment**

**Important:** Payment cannot proceed until staff have finalized the box sizes and shipping quote (Step 3). The system enforces this — if the shipping quote has not been set, the payment button will not be available.

Two payment modes exist:

| Mode | How It Works |
|---|---|
| **Counter Payment** (default) | Customer pays cash or uses a standalone card machine. Staff selects the payment method (Cash / Card / Other) and clicks "Confirm Payment Received." |
| **Online Payment** (if enabled) | Customer sees a "Pay Online" button on their phone and pays via Stripe. The amount is the finalized shipping quote. |

Once paid, the customer is free to leave the store.

**Step 5: Back Office Packing and Shipping**

1. Go to **Store → Back Office Packing** (shop.houseofspells.com/store/shipping/backoffice)
2. The packing queue lists all paid orders
3. **Scan Received** — enter your name and confirm receipt of the order (chain of custody)
4. **Check Items** — tick each item as verified
5. **Seal Package** — confirm all items are present and the box is sealed
6. **Weigh & Generate Label** — enter the package weight and generate a carrier shipping label (via Shippo)
7. **Verify Barcodes** — scan the carrier tracking barcode to verify it matches the HOS order
8. **Carrier Collected** — mark the package as handed to the carrier

**Shipping Order Status Flow:**

Customer Details Required → Awaiting Payment → Paid → Packing → Packed → Label Created → Ready for Pickup → Handed to Carrier

### 4.6 How Lightspeed POS Syncs with the Platform

The HOS Marketplace integrates with Lightspeed POS to keep online and in-store data synchronized:

| What Syncs | Direction | How Often |
|---|---|---|
| Products | Platform → POS | When products are assigned to a store |
| Inventory | Both directions | In-store sales update platform stock; online sales update POS stock; admin-triggered reconciliation |
| Customers | Platform → POS | Lightweight data (name, email, phone) for lookup |
| Sales | POS → Platform | Every in-store sale flows to the platform for reporting, loyalty, and analytics |
| Loyalty points | Platform manages | POS calls the platform at checkout for earn/redeem |

This means a customer's loyalty balance is always the same whether they check it online or in-store.

2. Choose between two tabs:
   - **Browse Catalog** — list an existing product (no approval needed)
   - **Submit New Product** — submit a completely new product (requires approval)

**Submit New Product — Step by Step**

1. Enter **product name** (the system automatically checks for duplicates)
2. Write a detailed **description**
3. Optionally enter SKU, barcode, and EAN numbers
4. Set **pricing**:
   - Selling price (required)
   - Currency (USD or EUR)
   - Trade price, RRP (optional)
   - Stock quantity (required)
   - Tax rate (optional)
5. Choose **categorization**:
   - Select a product category from the tree
   - Select a fandom from the dropdown
   - Add tags (comma-separated)
6. Upload at least one **product image** (JPEG, PNG, GIF, or WebP; max 10 MB each, up to 4 at a time)
   - Alternatively, paste an image URL
   - Add descriptive alt text for each image
7. Optionally add **product variations** (e.g., Size: Small/Medium/Large)
8. Click **Submit Product**

**Browse Catalog — Quick Listing**

If the product already exists in the HOS catalog:

1. Go to the "Browse Catalog" tab
2. Search or filter by fandom
3. Find the product and click **List as Vendor**
4. Set your selling price and stock quantity
5. Click **List Product** — the product is listed **instantly** with no approval needed

**Approval Pipeline**

After you submit a new product, it passes through these review stages:

Submitted → Under Review → Procurement Review → Finance Review → Catalog Enrichment → Marketing Review → Content Review → Product Created

| Stage | Who Reviews | What They Check |
|---|---|---|
| Procurement | Procurement Team | Product sourcing validation, quality check |
| Finance | Finance Team | Pricing and margins |
| Catalog | Catalog Team | Enriches product data, assigns proper taxonomy |
| Marketing | Marketing Team | Adds marketing content and positioning |
| Content | Content Team | Final review and product activation |

If your submission is **rejected** at any stage:
- You receive a notification explaining the reason
- You can correct the issues and **resubmit** the product

**Duplicate Detection**

When you type a product name, SKU, barcode, or EAN, the system automatically checks for duplicates:

| Match Type | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Your Active Products (red) | You already sell this product — no need to submit again |
| Your Pending Submissions (orange) | You already have a pending submission for this product |
| Catalogue Matches (amber) | The product exists in the catalog — use "List as Vendor" instead |

## 11. Gift Cards

Gift cards let customers give the gift of House of Spells to friends and family.

### 11.1 Types of Gift Cards

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Digital Gift Card** | A code delivered electronically — usable at online checkout |
| **Physical Gift Card** | A printed card with a code — usable at online checkout |

### 11.2 Purchasing a Gift Card

1. Go to **shop.houseofspells.com/gift-cards/purchase**
2. Choose a denomination: $25, $50, $100, $250, or $500
3. Optionally enter a recipient name and email
4. Optionally add a personal message
5. Complete payment via Stripe
6. The gift card code is delivered to the recipient (or to you)

### 11.3 Viewing Your Gift Cards

1. Go to **shop.houseofspells.com/gift-cards**
2. See all gift cards associated with your account
3. Click on a card to see its details: balance, status, and usage history
4. Gift card detail page: **shop.houseofspells.com/gift-cards/{id}**

### 11.4 Redeeming a Gift Card at Checkout

1. Add items to your cart and proceed to checkout
2. On the **payment page**, enter your gift card code in the "Gift Card" field
3. Click **Apply**
4. The gift card balance is applied toward your order total:
   - If the gift card covers the full amount — no additional payment needed
   - If partial — pay the remaining balance by card
5. After use, the gift card balance is reduced accordingly

### 11.5 Gift Card Statuses

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **ACTIVE** | The card has a remaining balance and can be used |
| **REDEEMED** | The balance has been fully used |
| **EXPIRED** | The card has passed its expiry date |
| **CANCELLED** | The card was cancelled by an admin |

### 11.6 Combining Gift Cards with Loyalty Points

Yes, you can use both on the same order:
1. At checkout, redeem loyalty points first (this reduces the order total)
2. On the payment page, apply your gift card code to the reduced amount
3. Pay any remaining balance by card

### 11.7 Gift Card Refunds

If an order paid with a gift card is cancelled, the gift card balance is **automatically restored**. An admin can also manually refund a gift card redemption if needed.
